# Functional requirements

Version 0.2 | 8 September 2026 | Owner: Dewald Allers | Status: **Draft for review**

Sources and need IDs: [Part 2 index](README.md). Detailed proposals P-01 to P-04 are in [decisions to confirm](decisions-to-confirm.md). Each AC link leads to observable criteria, not an executed test.

| ID | Requirement statement | Source / stakeholder | Priority and reason | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR-001 | The system shall create a service request from valid required information and return its unique reference; invalid submissions shall identify the fields requiring correction without creating a request. | Master section 3 requester, p. 7; NEED-P2-01; proposed fields/reference in P-01 | Must Have - starting point of the service workflow. | [AC-FR-001](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-001) |
| FR-002 | The system shall allow a requester to select a request category only from the approved category list. | Master section 3 requester, p. 7; NEED-P2-01; P-01 | Must Have - controlled categorisation is explicit. | [AC-FR-002](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-002) |
| FR-003 | The system shall let a requester view a list of their previously submitted requests, with each request's reference, title, submission time and current status. | Master section 3 requester, p. 7; NEED-P2-01; P-01/P-03 | Must Have - provides current status and request history. | [AC-FR-003](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-003) |
| FR-004 | The system shall provide the requester with feedback identifying the change and outcome when their request is accepted, rejected, updated, resolved or closed. | Master section 3 requester, p. 7; NEED-P2-01; proposed channel/timing in P-04 | Must Have - users must understand progress and outcome. | [AC-FR-004](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-004) |
| FR-005 | The system shall let authorised staff view their permitted request queue and the recorded details of a selected request. | Master section 3 staff, p. 7; NEED-P2-02; access scope in P-03 | Must Have - staff need relevant work and detail. | [AC-FR-005](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-005) |
| FR-006 | The system shall let authorised staff filter their permitted requests by category, status, or both together. | Master section 3 staff, p. 7; NEED-P2-02 | Must Have - a bounded way to find relevant work; chosen from the brief's search/filter/sort alternatives. | [AC-FR-006](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-006) |
| FR-007 | The system shall let authorised staff accept responsibility for a request or assign it to an eligible staff member, and display its current responsible member. | Master section 3 staff, p. 7; NEED-P2-02; P-03 | Must Have - prevents unclear ownership. | [AC-FR-007](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-007) |
| FR-008 | The system shall permit authorised staff to change a request's status only through the agreed transitions and with their required information. | Master section 3 staff, p. 7; NEED-P2-02; proposed transitions in P-02 | Must Have - controls acceptance, rejection, progress, resolution and closure. | [AC-FR-008](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-008) |
| FR-009 | The system shall let authorised staff record actions, comments, an agreed due date where available, and resolution information against the relevant request, retaining the author and recording time. | Master sections 2-3, pp. 6-7; NEED-P2-02/03; due-date proposal P-02; feedback visibility P-04 | Must Have - records the work and supplies due-date evidence for overdue reporting. | [AC-FR-009](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-009) |
| FR-010 | The system shall show authorised management request counts and matching request lists for open, overdue, resolved and closed work, filterable by category and status. | Master section 3 management, p. 7; NEED-P2-03; overdue/open meanings in P-02 | Must Have - directly supports oversight and service analysis. | [AC-FR-010](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-010) |
| FR-011 | The system shall make a request's submission, ownership changes, status changes and recorded actions available as a chronological history to authorised viewers, with the actor, time and change recorded. | Master sections 2-3, pp. 6-7; NEED-P2-02/03; visibility in P-03 | Must Have - addresses weak accountability and the missing lifecycle record. | [AC-FR-011](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-011) |
| FR-012 | The system shall let authorised staff order their permitted request list by submission time, oldest first. | Master section 3 staff, p. 7; NEED-P2-02 | Should Have - helps inspect ageing work; FR-006 already satisfies basic request finding. | [AC-FR-012](acceptance-criteria.md#ac-fr-012) |

FR-003 covers the brief's list/history of submitted requests. FR-011 is a different concept: the event history of one request. Requirement priority is not the operational urgency of an individual service request; no separate urgency-scoring feature is committed here.
