# First use check results for NFR-004

Version 1.0 | 16 October 2026 | Owner: Tristan Els | Status: **approved when its pull request merges**

On 16 October 2026 I ran the first use check with five people. The [check sheet](nfr-004-check.md) says how it should run, and this page says what happened. Where I did not follow the sheet, the page says so, and it says what that means for the result.

## Who

Five adults who are not on the team: four family members and my girlfriend. None of them had used the pages before. I write them as P1 to P5 and I hold their names privately.

## What I did

I sat with each person at the requester page. At the start I explained what the pages are for and what should happen. Then I asked them to send a request and find its status. I did not help them while they did it.

## Results

| Person | Completed both tasks | Help during the tasks | Under five minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Yes | None after the explanation at the start | Yes, by my clock check |
| P2 | Yes | None after the explanation at the start | Yes, by my clock check |
| P3 | Yes | None after the explanation at the start | Yes, by my clock check |
| P4 | Yes | None after the explanation at the start | Yes, by my clock check |
| P5 | Yes | None after the explanation at the start | Yes, by my clock check |

I looked at the clock before each session and again when the person finished. In every session fewer than five minutes had passed. I did not write the times down.

## Where this differs from the sheet

- The sheet says I do not explain the pages. I explained what the pages are for and what should happen, before the tasks.
- I did not write down a time for each person. I have the observation and not the numbers.
- I made no screen recording and no notes during the sessions.

AC-NFR-004 asks for timing and a record of any help. Because of these three differences the result cannot confirm the pass rule.

## What it shows and what it does not

It shows that five people who had not used the pages completed both tasks quickly, and none of them needed help after the explanation at the start.

It does not show that four of five people finish unaided in under five minutes, because I did not measure the times and I explained the pages first. It does not show how members of the public would do. Family members and a girlfriend are not a random sample, and they may go easier on me than a stranger would.

## Result

T-NFR-004-01 reads **Partly**. All five completed both tasks, and the pass rule is not confirmed. NFR-004 stays a proposed target under DEC-010.

To close it I would run the check again with five new people, write each time down during the session, and give no explanation beyond the wording of the tasks. That is mine to do if time allows.
