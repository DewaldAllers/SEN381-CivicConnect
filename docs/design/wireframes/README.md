# Wireframes

One wireframe for each page of the client in [app/frontend](../../../app/frontend). They show the layout and the labels each role sees, in plain greyscale. The colours and the photo live in the [stylesheet](../../../app/frontend/css/style.css), not here.

| Wireframe | Page | Who uses it | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [sign-in.png](sign-in.png) | `index.html` | Everyone | Sign in, then go to the page for your role |
| [requester.png](requester.png) | `requester.html` | Requester | FR-001, FR-002, FR-003, FR-004 |
| [staff.png](staff.png) | `staff.html` | Staff | FR-005 to FR-009, FR-011, FR-012 |
| [management.png](management.png) | `manager.html` | Management | FR-010 |

The names, categories and dates in the wireframes are sample values. The real category list comes from the seeded categories in the database.
