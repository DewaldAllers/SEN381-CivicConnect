## Evidence limitations

The Master Brief resolves the original scenario gap. It establishes requester, staff and management capabilities, but not complete detailed business rules or numeric service targets. The [Part 2 proposals](requirements/decisions-to-confirm.md) distinguish derived detail from source facts. No stakeholder interviews or real-world load measurements have been performed in this work.

Assignment 1 uses concise causal explanations, tables and lifecycle reasoning. This style informs the new structure. Its research claims and citations have not been independently reverified for M1; references include inconsistent years and incomplete entries. No stack scores or previous AI verification claims have been imported as M1 facts.
