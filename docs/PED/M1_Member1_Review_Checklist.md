# CivicConnect M1 Member 1 Review Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist supports the review and integration of the Member 1 contribution into the CivicConnect PED v1.0.

## Member 1 Areas to Review

- [ ] Problem & Business Need is consistent with the CivicConnect project brief.
- [ ] Stakeholder analysis identifies the main stakeholder groups and their needs.
- [ ] Stakeholder conflicts and competing expectations are documented.
- [ ] Scope baseline is consistent with the agreed CivicConnect business capabilities.
- [ ] In-scope, out-of-scope and deferred items are clearly identified.
- [ ] Project constraints are documented.
- [ ] Constraint trade-offs and ripple effects are explained.
- [ ] Team Working Agreement reflects the team's agreed working process.

## Cross-Team Consistency Checks

Before PED v1.0 is baselined, the team should verify that:

- [ ] Member 1 scope is consistent with Member 2 requirements.
- [ ] Stakeholder needs can be traced to relevant requirements.
- [ ] Member 1 constraints are considered by Member 3's risk register.
- [ ] Risks identified by Member 3 do not contradict the agreed scope.
- [ ] Decisions and deferred items are consistent across the PED.
- [ ] GitHub governance supports the agreed team working process.

## Baseline Readiness

The complete PED v1.0 should be reviewed by all three team members before baseline sign-off.

Final baseline approval is a team responsibility.
